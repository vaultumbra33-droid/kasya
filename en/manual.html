1. Getting started

Create your account

Open KASYA and follow the steps to create your account. Your identity is generated on your own phone: it is not registered with a phone number or an email.

Password and access PIN (optional)

In the first step of account creation you can type a numeric password. If you do, that same code works as your PIN: KASYA asks for it when you open the app. If you leave it empty, the app opens without a PIN and you can turn it on later in Settings > PIN lock. The next steps (username and profile) can also be skipped.

Write it down somewhere safe. KASYA has no server that can reset it for you.

Language

KASYA is available in English and Spanish. Change it in Settings > Language or on the welcome screen. The Terms and conditions are in Settings.

Keep KASYA connected

KASYA does not use push-notification servers. To receive messages and calls while the app is closed, it must keep running. When Keep KASYA connected appears, accept and, on the next screen, allow it to ignore battery optimization.

2. The main screen

The bottom bar has five sections:

  • Chats: your conversations, with a search bar and the filters Unread, Favorites and Groups.
  • Calls: the history of incoming, outgoing and missed calls.
  • Groups: your groups and the button to create a new one.
  • Explore: channels and stores.
  • Contacts: your contact list, your QR code and the scanner.

Favorites: long-press a conversation and choose Favorite. You can then find it with the Favorites filter.

Settings: open them from the main screen menu. PIN, storage, export and more are there.

Connection status: KASYA shows Connecting…, Connected (with the number of connections) or Offline.

3. Adding contacts

With a QR code

  1. Go to Contacts and open My QR / scan code.
  2. Show your QR to the other person, or scan theirs.
  3. The contact request is created and you can start chatting.

Searching by name or ID

In Contacts, search by name or paste the person's ID.

Contact requests

When someone wants to add you, the request appears under Contact requests, in the menu. You can accept or decline it.

Sharing a contact

In the Contacts list, open a contact and choose Share contact to send their ID, or Open chat to write to them.

4. Chatting

Sending messages and files

Open a conversation, type and send. With the attach button you can send photos, videos and documents.

Actions on a message

Long-press a message to see: Reply, Copy, Edit message, Message history and Delete message.

Sending your location

From the attach menu choose location and decide between Send my current location (one time) or Share live location. It is shared only if you choose it.

Photos without location

When you send a JPEG photo, KASYA removes the GPS position and the device and author data from the copy that is sent. Your original photos are not modified.

Disappearing messages

In the conversation menu open Disappearing messages and choose: Off, 24 hours, 7 days, 15 days or 30 days. When the time is up, messages and files are deleted from the chat.

Each phone deletes its own copy. A screenshot, or a modified app, cannot be prevented.

Read receipts

In Settings you can turn off Read receipts: others will not know when you read their messages. You still see when they read yours.

Conversation details

Open the details to see Members, Files (photos, videos, documents) and Settings.

5. Groups

  1. Go to Groups and tap Create new group.
  2. Choose a name and the contacts who will take part.
  3. Write, share files and make calls as in any chat.

Under Members you see who takes part. Groups also appear with the Groups filter in Chats.

6. Calls and video calls

  • Call: open a chat and tap the call or video call button.
  • Answer: a notification appears with accept and decline options.
  • Group calls: several people on the same call. The grid adapts to whether your phone is vertical or horizontal.
  • History: under Calls you see incoming, outgoing and missed calls.

Calls are end-to-end encrypted. To receive them while the app is closed, KASYA must keep running (see step 1).

7. Channels, stores and news

In Explore there are three tabs: Channels, Stores and News.

Create a channel

  1. In Explore tap Create channel.
  2. Type the name, a description (optional) and choose a category: News, Sports, Music, Entertainment, Technology, Education, Deals or Other.
  3. Tap Create. Only you, as the administrator, publish; those who follow it read.

If you make it public, anyone with the link can follow it. Use Share link to send it.

Follow a channel

  • Among the Suggested in Explore, or with Follow by link: paste the link kasya://follow/… and tap Follow.
  • The request reaches the owner and is approved if the channel is public and the owner is online.
  • To leave: Unfollow. If you do not want to see it anymore: Block channel or Block owner.
  • In Settings, Blocked channels lets you unblock what you blocked.

Stores

  1. On the Stores tab, tap Create store and give it a name and category.
  2. With Add product add a name, reference price, description and a photo.
  3. Anyone who sees a product can tap Ask the owner, and a chat opens with the message ready.

Prices are for reference. KASYA does not process payments: whatever is agreed is settled between the two people.

News

The News tab shows headlines read directly from each outlet's RSS feed, without going through a server of ours. Pick a region (World, Mexico, Latin America, USA, Europe, Russia, China, Asia, Middle East, Africa or Markets). Stories are grouped by day (Today, Yesterday and earlier dates); KASYA keeps 7 days of headlines on your phone. Tap a story to open it in your browser. State-funded outlets carry a label.

When you open that tab, the outlets can see your IP address, as on any website.

8. Privacy and security

PIN lock

In Settings you can review your PIN, change it or remove it. The Ask for PIN field decides when it is asked again when you return to the app: immediately, or after 10 seconds, 1, 5, 15 or 30 minutes away.

Emergency PIN

Set a second PIN in Settings, different from yours. If someone forces you to open KASYA and you type that PIN on the lock screen:

  • KASYA erases everything without asking: accounts, messages, photos, documents and settings.
  • The app restarts and opens empty, as if it were new.
  • It cannot be undone.
Files you exported outside the app are not deleted.

End-to-end encryption

Your messages and calls are encrypted: only you and your contact have the keys. There is no central server that stores your conversations.

9. Storage and backup

Storage

In Settings > Storage you see what the app has downloaded (photos, videos, audio and documents) and you can delete a file, a category or everything. It also disappears from the conversation and cannot be recovered.

Export / back up

In Settings > Export / back up you choose what to save: Channels, Store, Contacts and Chats. A file is prepared that you can share or save wherever you prefer.

Take care of that file: it contains your conversations without the app's protection.

10. Common problems

I do not receive messages or calls while the app is closed

KASYA does not use server notifications. Go to your phone's battery settings and allow KASYA to ignore optimization. On some brands you also need to allow auto-start.

It says "Offline" or "Connecting…"

Check your internet (Wi-Fi or data). When the network returns, KASYA reconnects by itself. If it stays the same, close and reopen the app.

I forgot my PIN

KASYA has no way to reset it: there is no server or recovery email. That is why it is a good idea to write it down somewhere safe.

Someone does not receive my request or my message

On a network with no central server, some actions complete when both people are online. Wait for the other person to open KASYA.

I want to erase everything from my phone

Use the emergency PIN (section 8) or delete the downloaded files from Settings > Storage.

Is KASYA free and safe?

KASYA is based on Jami, a free software project. You can check the APK: compare its SHA-256 fingerprint with the one on the download page and scan it on VirusTotal.
